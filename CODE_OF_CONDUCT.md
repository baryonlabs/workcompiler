# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and specific; review the work, not the person; no harassment,
discrimination or personal attacks; disagreements are settled with evidence — a failing test, a
benchmark, a trace.

Reports: **hello@baryon.ai**. Maintainers will respond within 5 business days and keep reports
confidential. Consequences range from a warning to removal from the project spaces.
